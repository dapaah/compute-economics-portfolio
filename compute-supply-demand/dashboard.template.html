When can capacity meet requested service?

Compare modeled energized additions with requested-service cohorts, then follow merchant capacity through readiness and occupation. This is an aggregate scenario, not measured demand fulfillment or a forecast of chip availability.

Opening cohorts, geographic shares and occupancy ramp are assumptions. Most inherited sources remain unverified. Transformers constrain readiness time; there is no transformer throughput ceiling.

Alternative BTM is an unverified equipment-scope assumption for the aero route. Shares and turbine allocations stay fixed; inspect the component table.

Control ranges are exploratory, not confidence intervals. Full inputs and provenance appear below.

Energized additions and requested-service year

Teal bars: energized IT capacity · dark line: base requested service · dashed lines: low/high booking-growth scenarios.

Signed North America bookings are allocated to the US, then split by requested-service lag. They are not added to capex-implied demand. Annual supply minus requested service can be positive or negative; it is not a matched-customer backlog.

What sets each pathway’s readiness?

Lead time is the maximum elapsed readiness milestone from announcement. Milestones are treated as parallel; this is not a sequential project schedule. Turbine ceilings use an inherited industry range and assumed allocations, fixed over the horizon. Unused frame allocations do not transfer between turbine types.

Customer funding shares over time

Assumed ownership paths, not observed customer consumption. Aggregate capital growth is fixed across scenarios. The bookings chart above is a separate lens and does not change with funding mix.

US merchant occupation: a separate capital-demand lens

Readiness is capacity able to accept occupancy. Occupied additions match ready capacity with capital-implied service requests and prior backlog, assuming fungibility. Actual live IT electrical load is not measured or inferred. Existing occupied fleet, cancellations and retirements are excluded. Revenue output is a year-end annual run-rate proxy, not recognized sales.

Validation status

The former benchmark is year-end primary-market inventory, not annual US delivery. It cannot validate this model. No compatible historical observation series is supplied. The Python backtest interface requires same-year US annual energized additions and a frozen pre-period snapshot.
