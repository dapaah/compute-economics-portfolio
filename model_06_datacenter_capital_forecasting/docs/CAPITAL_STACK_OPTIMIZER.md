# Capital Stack Optimizer

Phase 3 compares five illustrative funding architectures against the same validated base-case operating forecast.

## Sources modeled

- Sponsor equity
- Project / construction debt
- Equipment financing
- Customer prepayments
- Grants and other non-dilutive support

## Decision outputs

- Sponsor equity requirement and estimated dilution
- Debt draw, interest during construction, debt service, and ending balance
- CADS and rolling 12-month DSCR
- Coverage breach months against structure-specific thresholds
- Minimum cash and incremental funding requirement
- Levered equity IRR and unlevered portfolio NPV

## Interpretation

The optimizer is a decision framework, not a financing recommendation. All structures use synthetic assumptions. The coverage analysis intentionally reveals when leverage that appears attractive on equity returns is unsupported by operating cash flow during ramp.

Equipment financing is included within aggregate debt draws for cash-flow purposes and separately identified for source-mix disclosure. Grants and customer prepayments reduce sponsor equity contributions without creating debt service.
